Upload files

Two CSV files: your internal orders export and the PSP / acquirer payments export. Delimiter and encoding are detected automatically; amounts are handled as exact decimals.

How it works

  1. Upload orders.csv and payments.csv.
  2. Map Order ID → merchant_reference, Amount → amount, Currency → currency, Date → payment_date (suggested automatically).
  3. Run reconciliation: exact match by reference, then amount and currency comparison, duplicate detection.
  4. Review the summary and the exceptions table; export CSV or XLSX.
